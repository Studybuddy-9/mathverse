CBSE CURRICULUM 2026–27

Latest Class 11 syllabus

Mathematics • Subject Code 041

Exam: 3 hours • 80 marks theory • 20 marks internal assessment.

Sets and Functions: 23 marks • Algebra: 25 • Coordinate Geometry: 12 • Calculus: 8 • Statistics and Probability: 12. CBSE says there is no fixed chapter-wise weightage, so cover every chapter.

Formative-only topics listed by CBSE

  • Practical union/intersection problems, composition of functions and general trigonometric equation solutions.
  • Mathematical Induction; polar form of complex numbers; graphical linear inequalities in two variables.
  • General and middle binomial terms; special sums of natural numbers, squares and cubes.
  • Normal form and general equation of a line; 3D section formula.
  • Chain rule; random experiments, outcomes and sample spaces.
These topics are marked formative-only in the CBSE document. Confirm school-level coverage with your teacher.
