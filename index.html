COMPUTING

FINANCIAL LITERACY

MATHS EXHIBITION

Project ideas for students

1. Graphs around us

Collect simple daily-life data, plot it with the X–Y graph tool and explain the pattern.

2. Probability experiment

Use a classroom-friendly coin or dice experiment and compare experimental and theoretical probability.

3. Conics in architecture

Find circles, parabolas and ellipses in buildings and everyday objects. Label their mathematical features.

4. Statistics of a class

Use anonymous, non-sensitive sample data to demonstrate tables, averages and charts.

How to present a maths project

Aim → concept → method or model → calculations and observations → conclusion → learning outcomes → references.
